Capabilities

Built to sit under skin, not on top of attention.

Three subsystems, one coin-sized implant. Each does one job well and hands off cleanly to the next.

Sense

Cortical read array

A 128-channel array reads intent signals directly from the motor cortex, filtering noise in hardware before anything reaches software.

Translate

On-device inference

A low-power model turns raw signal into discrete commands locally. Nothing leaves the implant until you choose to pair a device.

Act

Haptic return channel

Machines talk back through the same link — pressure, texture, and proximity, rendered as nerve-native feedback instead of a buzz.

How it works

From intent to action in four steps.

Step 1

Placement

A 40-minute outpatient procedure sets the array beneath the scalp. No external hardware, no visible scar within six weeks.

Step 2

Calibration

Two weeks of guided use lets the on-device model learn your signal patterns. Most users stop noticing the calibration app by day nine.

Step 3

Pairing

Pair with any Axiom-compatible device over a short-range encrypted link. Pairing is revocable from the implant at any time.

Step 4

Daily use

Intent becomes input. Think a motion, the paired device receives it — typing, steering, gripping, all without a hand in between.
